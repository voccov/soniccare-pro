Why 48 kHz is not the whole story

What SVR means

SVR compares the high-frequency "Ssss" band (3–6.5 kHz) with the low-frequency "Ahhh" band (150–800 Hz). A lower value means less high-frequency energy in that microphone.

What we measured

Left and right SVR, the high-frequency difference, and the active input route. These are example values for this blueprint.

What we didn't measure

Absolute sound pressure, firmware, serial, battery, or a hardware fault. This is a check, not a diagnosis.

Clean the right mesh

Use Apple's method: micellar water and a soft children's toothbrush. No liquids in the openings, no compressed air, no sharp tools.
  1. 1Take the right earbud outPlace it mesh-down on a clean table.
  2. 2Brush the meshMicellar water on a soft children's toothbrush. Light strokes only.
  3. 3Dry for two hoursLeave it out of the case, mesh-down, in a dry room.
  4. 4Test againCompare the same ear with the baseline.
