What SVR means

SVR compares the high-frequency "Ssss" band (3–6.5 kHz) with the low-frequency "Ahhh" band (150–800 Hz). A lower value means less high-frequency energy in that microphone.

What we measured

Left and right SVR, the high-frequency difference, and the active input route. These are example values for this mockup.

What we didn't measure

Absolute sound pressure, firmware, serial, battery, or a hardware fault. This is a check, not a diagnosis.
