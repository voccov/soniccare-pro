A two-minute check for muffled or uneven sound. We test each earbud and show what we measured.

Audio is processed on this phone. No uploads, no account, no recordings. Demo mode uses a synthetic tone and is labelled.

What this is

A relative check, not a lab test. It compares the left and right microphones and looks for high-frequency loss. Bluetooth speech audio is limited, so a result is a signal, not a diagnosis.

Care

Clean the mesh

Apple method

Clean only the meshes Apple highlights. Keep liquids out of the openings.

1
Stem ring

Primary call microphone at the bottom.

2
Speaker mesh

Under the silicone tip.

3
Outer ANC mesh

The black outer grille.

4
Inner pressure port

Do not poke it with tools.

Apple's method: micellar water with PEG-6 Caprylic/Capric Glycerides, a soft children's toothbrush, blot on a paper towel, rinse with distilled water, then dry for at least 2 hours. No ethanol on the mesh, no compressed air, no vacuum, no Blu-Tack, no sharp tools.

How to switch ears

Go back to iOS Settings, choose the other microphone, then return here and tap Reconnect input. The app cannot switch it for you.
